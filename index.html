Privacy Policy — Magnet.io

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy applies to the Android game Magnet.io (package name io.magnet.game), published by NanoPlayer. Contact: nanoinfo.app@gmail.com.

1. Data on your device

The game saves locally: player name, optional profile photo, coins, skins, settings, language, and mission progress.

2. Online play

Display name, skin, and match/chat messages may be sent through public MQTT relays so players can join a room. Do not put personal information in your name or chat.

3. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect advertising ID, device information, IP address, and ad interactions. Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Purchases

Optional remove-ads purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your card details.

5. Delete your data

Email nanoinfo.app@gmail.com with subject: Delete my Magnet.io data. On your phone: Settings → Apps → Magnet.io → Storage → Clear data. Uninstalling also deletes local data.
